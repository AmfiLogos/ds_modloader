# Dungeon Settlers Modded: crash reports, bug reports and privacy

This page tells you what a crash report and a bug report contain. It also tells you how to turn crash reports off.

## What we collect

A crash report can contain:

- The loader log (`dsload.log`).
- The game logs: `player.log` and the game's `ErrorLog.txt`.
- The multiplayer log (`rig.log`).
- Summaries of desync reports.
- The versions of the loader, of your mods and of the game build.
- Your operating system and your CPU type.
- A random install id. The loader makes this id. It is not a hardware id.

We do not collect a hardware id.

## What is removed before sending

Your computer removes these items before it sends the report:

- User names in folder paths, and your user name, full name and computer name anywhere in the text.
- Steam names and Steam ids.
- Player names.
- IP addresses.
- Email addresses and network card (MAC) addresses.

## Why we collect it

We use the reports to find and fix bugs, crashes, freezes, desyncs, failed joins and an empty game view.

## When a crash report is sent

- Only after you click Yes.
- Only after a crash, a freeze, a desync report, a failed join, or a multiplayer view that showed nothing.
- At most 3 reports per day.
- The report is sent at the next start of the game, or at the title screen. Never while you play.

## Bug reports

You can send a bug report from the game. A bug report contains:

- The text that you write.
- Your contact, for example a Discord name or an email address. Only if you type one. We use it only to ask you about the bug.
- A screenshot of the game, if you add one.
- The same logs, versions and install id as a crash report. The same items are removed from the logs before sending.

Your text and your contact are sent as you type them. Do not type data that you do not want to send.

A bug report is sent only when you click Send.

## Who can see a report

Only the maker of Dungeon Settlers Modded can see reports. Reports are kept in private storage and are never made public.

When a bug report arrives, a short notice goes to a private Discord channel of the maker. The notice contains the versions, your operating system and the first 120 characters of your text.

## How long we keep it

We keep each report (crash or bug, with its screenshot) for 90 days. Then it is deleted automatically.

## Your IP address

The server does not store your IP address with the report.

## How to turn it off

1. Go to the title screen.
2. Open Mods.
3. Turn off the switch "Send crash reports".

This page uses no tracking and no cookies.
